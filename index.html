Walking distance

Import from Apple Health (set up once)
Your iPhone counts walking distance all day. A Shortcut sends it to your private sync code, and this app picks it up whenever you open it. Nothing opens in Safari.
Your sync code:
Shortcut URL:
In your Park Walking Log Shortcut:
  1. Keep Find Health Samples, the Repeat with Text [Start Date]=[Value], and Combine Text with |.
  2. In the URL action, replace the address with the copied Shortcut URL, then add Combined Text right after data=.
  3. Delete Open URLs and add Get Contents of URL instead (it sends quietly).
  4. Optional: add Show Notification with Contents of URL to see "Saved 24 hours".
For every day: Automation → + → Time of Day 11:00 PM, Daily, Run Immediately → Park Walking Log.
